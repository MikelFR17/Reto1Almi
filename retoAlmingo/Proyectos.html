Our company's projects

Our project in progress

EcoBingo – Sustainability Challenge

EcoBingo is an interactive group game designed to promote environmental awareness through gamification. Players participate in a collective bingo activity where they complete challenges and answer questions related to sustainability, recycling and environmental protection.

Completed projects

FitLife – Healthy Lifestyle App

FitLife is a mobile application designed to help users improve their physical health and maintain a healthy lifestyle. It allows users to track their daily activities, set fitness goals and monitor their progress through personalized statistics.

EduSmart – Online Learning

EduSmart is an interactive online learning platform designed to make education more accessible and engaging. It offers users different courses, interactive quizzes and educational resources to improve their knowledge and learning experience.

GreenHome – Smart Energy

GreenHome is an innovative application designed to help households monitor and reduce their energy consumption. It provides real-time information about energy usage, personalized saving recommendations and tools to encourage more sustainable habits.

TravelGo – Smart Travel Planner

TravelGo is a smart travel planning application designed to help users discover destinations and organize their trips easily. It provides personalized travel recommendations, interactive maps and useful information about places to visit, helping users create customized itineraries and enjoy a better travel experience.

PetCare – Pet Management App

PetCare is a mobile application designed to help pet owners manage their pets' daily needs. Users can organize veterinary appointments, track vaccinations and receive reminders about their pets' health and wellbeing.
